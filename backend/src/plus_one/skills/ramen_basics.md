# Ramen basics — Tokyo

A working knowledge for the Producer / Joiner agents to use when ramen
is plausibly relevant to a user's request.

## Major styles

- **Tonkotsu** (豚骨) — opaque pork-bone broth. Originated in Fukuoka but
  widely available in Tokyo. Famous chains: Ichiran, Ippudo. Notable
  Tokyo independents: Afuri (yuzu-shio variant), Menya Itto.
- **Shoyu** (醤油) — soy-sauce-based clear-ish broth, the Tokyo classic.
  Try: Tsuta (Michelin), Nakiryu.
- **Shio** (塩) — salt-based, lightest of the bunch. Try: Menya Shono.
- **Miso** — fermented soybean paste base, originally from Sapporo.
- **Tsukemen** (つけ麺) — dipping noodles served separately from a
  concentrated broth. Try: Rokurinsha (Tokyo Ramen Street), TETSU.
- **Mazesoba / Aburasoba** — soup-less, noodles tossed in tare + oil.

## Reading a menu

- 大盛り (oomori) — large portion (usually +¥100-200).
- 替え玉 (kaedama) — extra noodle helping (tonkotsu shops).
- 味玉 (ajitama) — soft-boiled marinated egg (+¥100-150).
- チャーシュー — braised pork.
- メンマ — fermented bamboo shoots.

## Local-vs-tourist heuristics

Use these signals when classifying a shop. None alone is decisive.

- **Tourist-leaning**: English menu prominent, photos of the food on
  the wall, multiple guidebook stickers, location in Shibuya/Shinjuku/
  Asakusa core. Wait times consistently > 45 min suggest social-media
  fame more than quality.
- **Local-leaning**: Located 5-15 min walk from a non-major station,
  ticket machine (食券機) only with no English, regulars greeted by
  name, chefs older than 50 working alone or with one apprentice.
- **Both**: Michelin-listed shops draw both crowds; treat them as
  high-quality but expect tourist queues.

## Useful search prompts

- For local-leaning candidates: search Reddit r/ramen + r/japan with
  "Tokyo ramen Tabelog" or "Tokyo ramen recommendation locals".
- For Chinese-perspective Tokyo ramen: XHS query "东京 拉面 推荐"
  (Tokyo ramen recommendation) or "东京 隐藏 拉面" (Tokyo hidden ramen).
- For factual data (address, hours, Google rating): Google Places with
  the shop's romanized name + Tokyo as location_hint.

## Red flags

- Shop name appears in 5+ "must-try Tokyo ramen" listicles in 2024-2025
  but absent from /r/ramen power-user posts → likely SEO-optimized
  tourist trap.
- "Insta-famous" shops with theatrical presentation (e.g., flame-broiled
  toppings done table-side) often trade flavor for spectacle.
