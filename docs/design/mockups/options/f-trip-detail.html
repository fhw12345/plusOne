Menya Saimi.麺屋彩未

Sugamo · counter for four

A neighbourhood shio counter without an English menu. The English-speaking community describes the broth as thin, almost transparent; the Chinese-speaking community describes the room as the most peaceful one in town. Both are right. We recommend a weekday morning.

A polite recommendation against

Ichiran — Shibuya branch.

Shibuya · the chain you may know

The chain is fine; the location is not. Eight of ten Chinese sources recommend against this branch specifically — long queues, premium pricing for the same bowl. We would direct you instead to the Ueno branch, which both communities recommend without reservation.
