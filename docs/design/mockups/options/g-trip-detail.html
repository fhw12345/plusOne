Menya Saimi麺屋彩未

Sugamo · counter for four · no English menu

A quiet shio counter in the back streets of Sugamo. Reddit calls the broth thin, almost transparent; Xiaohongshu calls the room 「最安静的拉面店」 — the quietest in town. Both, agreeable. We’d send you on a weekday morning.

Ichiran — Shibuya branch

Shibuya · the chain you may know

The chain itself is well enough; the location is not. Eight in ten Chinese sources warn off this particular branch — long queues, premium pricing for an ordinary bowl. The Ueno branch is recommended without dispute.
