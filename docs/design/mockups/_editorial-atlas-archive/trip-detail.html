Menya Saimi.

Sugamo 12 sources You0.91 Yuna0.78

A quiet shio counter, no English menu. Locals on Reddit describe a “thin, almost transparent” broth and a chef who refuses to discuss the recipe. Chinese sources rate the value highly and note short queues on weekday mornings; English sources concede the technique but mark it “not novel enough” for a special trip — a recurring split worth knowing.

A tourist trap, gently

Ichiran Shibuya.

Shibuya 41 sources Skip

Eight of ten Chinese sources warn off this branch specifically (the chain itself is fine elsewhere). Long queues, premium pricing, no contact with broth-craft — the booth design is the brand. Worth knowing if it’s on your list.
