Tokyo東京

three days for Yuna & Wei — tonkotsu, no tourist traps, kissaten yes

Pinned12 MAY 2026 3 days · 11 stops
tl;dr Lean into Sugamo for ramen, Jimbocho for kissaten, Yanaka for the slow afternoon. Skip Shibuya for food, do it after dark only.

Day 1 12 May · mon

arrive late from haneda, drop bags in sugamo, eat the ramen you came for, sleep.

afternoon ~3pm

Sugamo Jizo-dori 巣鴨地蔵通り

Sugamo · Toshima · 6 sources

The arcade locals call "old-lady Harajuku." Mostly grannies in soft cardigans browsing crackers and red-pant shops. Good for a 40-minute stroll after you drop the bags — buy a senbei, peek into the temple at the end, do not buy the red underwear unless you're committing to the bit.

soft yes
evening 7pm sharp, get there 6:40

Menya Saimi 麺屋彩未

Sugamo · 12 sources agree

The one you actually came to Tokyo for. Eight-seat shio counter, no English menu, owner's wife slicing scallions the whole time. Locals on r/ramen call the broth "almost transparent." Order the regular shio + ajitama and shut up about it.

Yuna Wei

YES. this one.

Sanpei Sakaba 三平酒場

Sugamo · 8 sources

Walk five minutes from the ramen place. Late-shift salaryman territory — yakitori, simmered things, one-sho bottles of cheap sake. Feels like Midnight Diner without the camera. Wei: skip the shelled stuff (turban shell + bai-gai), the rest is yours.

Yuna Wei

go.
Day 2 13 May · tue

books, coffee, second-hand cameras, and a curry that's loud about being cheap.

morning open 10:00, go 10:05

Kissaten Nostalgia 純喫茶

Jimbocho · 5 sources

Mahogany the color of decades of cigarettes, a hand-drip pour-over that takes nine minutes on purpose, no wi-fi. The middle-aged owner talks to you in the language of his grinder. Yuna will sit here for an hour without checking her phone. Order the blend + the thick-cut toast.

Yuna Wei

for Yuna.

Jimbocho Book Town 神保町古書店街

Jimbocho · Chiyoda · 14 sources

~180 used-book stores in a 10-minute walking radius. Most stalls face north on purpose so the sun won't bleach the spines. Komiyama-shoten for photo books; Issei-do for cartography; the side alleys for the weird stuff. Bring cash, the older shops don't take cards.

Yuna Wei

essential.
afternoon lunch ~1pm, walk after

A-un 阿吽

Takadanobaba · 9 sources

Hokkaido-style soup curry in a student district. Cheap, generous, and five different Chinese reviewers landed independently on 4.5/5. Pick the chicken-and-veg + spice level 5 (out of 10) — anything higher is for show. One subway hop from Jimbocho.

Yuna Wei

worth the detour.

Yanaka Ginza 谷中銀座

Yanaka · Taito · 7 sources

A low-slung shopping street that survived the war. Croquette stall on the left as you come down the stairs ("yuyake dandan" — the "sunset stairs"). Cats everywhere, mostly indifferent. End at the cemetery, walk through, exit toward Nippori. The whole loop is about 90 quiet minutes.

Yuna Wei

yes, slowly.
evening after dark, ~8pm

Shibuya, after dark only 渋谷

Shibuya · 22 sources · food: skip

The crossing is worth fifteen minutes, no more. Go up to the Hikarie 11F bridge for the view without the crowd. Do not eat here — the ramen is a tourist racket (see Ichiran note in disagreements). Walk over to Nonbei Yokocho for one beer, then leave.

Yuna Wei

15 min & out.
Day 3 14 May · wed

slow market, museum nobody talks about, last bowl before the train.

morning 6:30am if you can

Tsukiji Outer Market 築地場外市場

Chuo · 18 sources · contested (see ticket)

Both EN and ZH sources agree on exactly one stand: the tamagoyaki place on the corner (Yamacho). Otherwise: it's a stage set now. Go at 6:30, eat one egg roll on a stick, photograph the tuna guy, leave before 8 when the bus tours arrive.

Yuna Wei

only the egg roll.
afternoon ~2pm

Nezu Museum 根津美術館

Minami-Aoyama · 6 sources

A Kengo Kuma building wrapped around a private collection of Japanese and Chinese antiquities. The garden out the back is the actual reason to go — koi pond, moss, four tea houses you can't enter but can peer at. Quiet enough to hear the bamboo. ¥1,500.

Yuna Wei

go.

Aoyama backstreets 青山裏通り

Minato · 4 sources

From the Nezu, wander west — past Prada Aoyama (the Herzog & de Meuron crystal block), into the side streets of small concept shops, end at Commune for an outdoor coffee. No plan, low stakes, good light around 4pm.

Yuna Wei

drift.
evening last meal, ~7pm

Ichiran — Ueno branch (not Shibuya) 一蘭 上野

Ueno · Taito · 11 sources · the resolved disagreement

ZH sources flag the Shibuya branch as a tourist racket; the Ueno one runs the same kitchen with about half the line and twice the locals. Order the classic tonkotsu, 1× kaedama, hardness "kata," richness "futsuu." If the queue's over 20 minutes anyway, just walk to Afuri at the Atre and call it.

Yuna Wei

end on this.
